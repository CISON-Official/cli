2026 PRS Professional Development Workshop

Dear Member,

The Chartered Institute of Statisticians of Nigeria (CISON) is pleased to invite you to the 2026 Planning, Research and Statistics (PRS) Professional Development Workshop.

This workshop is designed to equip participants with modern skills in statistics, artificial intelligence, and computational tools, bridging traditional statistical analysis with generative AI and advanced data-driven techniques.

Participants will have the opportunity to learn directly from distinguished experts, including:

  • Dr. Peter O. Ohue – Ambassador and Computational Neuroscience Instructor, Neuromatch Academy, Oregon, USA
  • Prof. Jude C. Obi – Professor of Statistics and Data Science, Chukwuemeka Odumegwu Ojukwu University, Nigeria

The workshop will cover:

  • Generative AI for predictive analysis
  • Advances in computational and statistical tools
  • Regression-based data analysis using R programming

Date: 2nd – 6th March, 2026
Time: 9:00 AM daily
Venue: University of Lagos, Akoka, Yaba, Lagos

We encourage professionals, organizations, and students to come, participate, and register early to benefit from this impactful learning experience.
