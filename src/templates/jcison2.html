Scope

The Journal welcomes submissions in Theoretical and Applied Statistics across all fields of study and research. We encourage contributions that advance statistical science and its practical applications.

About the Journal

JCISON is published both online and in print. It operates as a fully open-access journal with a rigorous double-blind peer review process, annual publication cycle, and zero Article Processing Charges for all authors.
