Subject: Resolution of Account Activation and Email Issues

Dear Member,

We are writing to apologize for the technical difficulties encountered with our membership portal over the last few days. We understand that many of you were unable to complete your registration because the system failed to send the necessary activation emails. This was due to essential system maintenance and a temporary breakdown in our automated mail server.

Your account has now been manually activated. You may now log in directly using the credentials you provided during registration.

Login Instructions:
  1. Go to the CISON Membership Portal.
  2. Enter your registered email address and your password.
  3. Once logged in, you will have full access to your dashboard to complete your profile, pay annual dues, or download resources.

We sincerely regret the inconvenience this caused and appreciate your patience as we worked to resolve these maintenance hurdles. If you encounter any further issues logging in, please reach out to our support team at support@cison.org.ng.

Thank you for your continued dedication to the Institute.

Best regards,
System Analyst
Chartered Institute of Statisticians of Nigeria (CISON)
