INVITATION TO THE 3RD INTERNATIONAL CONFERENCE AND 3RD PRE-CONFERENCE WORKSHOP OF THE CHARTERED INSTITUTE OF STATISTICIANS OF NIGERIA (CISON)

On behalf of the Governing Council of the Chartered Institute of Statisticians of Nigeria (CISON), it is with great pleasure that we invite you to participate in our 3rd International Conference themed “From Legacy to Leadership: 50 Years of Promoting Statistical Development in Nigeria.”

This event will take place at the Chida International Hotels Event Center, 224 Solomon Lar Road, Utako District, Abuja, Nigeria from 14th to 16th October 2026. The conference will be preceded by the 3rd Pre-Conference Workshop on “Advanced Techniques for Spatial Data Analysis and Interactive Visualization” taking place from 12th to 16th October 2026 at the same venue.

This year’s conference marks a historic milestone for the Institute as we celebrate 50 years of impactful existence, having been initially created and established as the Nigerian Statistical Association (NSA) in 1976. We evolved over the years, gloriously transiting into the Chartered Institute of Statisticians of Nigeria (CISON) following the CISON Act 2022.

The conference will provide a platform for reflection, knowledge exchange, and strategic dialogue on the future of statistics in national development among others, while the workshop is specially designed to equip participants with cutting-edge skills in spatial data handling and visualization, essential for modern statistical practice and policy-making.

It is a time to honour our past achievements and shape the future of our profession. The week-long event will feature the 3rd Pre-conference Workshop; Scientific Sessions; Panel Discussion; 50th Anniversary Celebration; Transition and Admission of Fellows; Induction of New Members; among others.

Note: The membership transition fee is a one-off fee payable by former Nigerian Statistical Association (NSA) members transiting to CISON. New members are not required to pay the transition fee. However, please note that these fees do not cover accommodation and transportation of participants during the events from 12th to 16th October, 2026.

Registrations and payments of all funds should be made by visiting the CISON’s website at www.cison.org.ng and following the instructions therein. Kindly note that the “Early Bird” conference payment as indicated in the table above expires on June 30, 2026.

We appreciate your commitment and continued membership of the Institute. On behalf of the Governing Council and entire members of the Chartered Institute of Statisticians of Nigeria, please accept the assurances of our highest regards.

Thank you.
