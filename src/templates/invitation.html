INVITATION TO THE 3RD INTERNATIONAL CONFERENCE AND 3RD PRE-CONFERENCE WORKSHOP OF THE CHARTERED INSTITUTE OF STATISTICIANS OF NIGERIA (CISON)

On behalf of the Governing Council of the Chartered Institute of Statisticians of Nigeria (CISON), it is with great pleasure that we invite you to participate in our 3rd International Conference themed "From Legacy to Leadership: 50 Years of Promoting Statistical Development in Nigeria." This event will take place from 14th to 16th October 2026, in Abuja FCT, Nigeria. This will be preceded by its 3rd Pre-Conference Workshop on "Advanced Techniques for Spatial Data Analysis and Interactive Visualization" taking place from 12th to 13th October 2026 at the same venue. Both events will run from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm daily.

This year's conference marks a historic milestone for the Institute as we celebrate 50 years in existence, having been initially created and established as the Nigerian Statistical Association (NSA) in 1976 and have evolved over the years, gloriously transiting into the Chartered Institute of Statisticians of Nigeria (CISON).

The conference will provide a platform for reflection, knowledge exchange, and strategic dialogue on the future of statistics in national development among others, while the workshop is specially designed to equip participants with cutting-edge skills in spatial data handling and visualization, essential for modern statistical practice and policy-making.
